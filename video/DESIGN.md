# Trofy Video Design Identity

## Style Prompt
Midnight Sleek: A high-contrast, dark, and technical aesthetic. Focus on deep blacks, vibrant cian glows, and smooth, purposeful motion. The feeling should be "Elite Sports Management Tool".

## Colors
- **Background**: `#050505` (Pure black depth)
- **Primary**: `#00F5FF` (Vibrant Cian)
- **Accent**: `#64FFDA` (Emerald Mint)
- **Text Primary**: `#FFFFFF`
- **Text Secondary**: `#94a3b8` (Slate gray for labels)

## Typography
- **Headings**: 'Outfit', sans-serif (Bold, 700+)
- **Body**: 'Inter', sans-serif (Medium, 500)

## Motion Rules
- **Entrances**: Use `power3.out` for fast, snappy arrivals.
- **Glows**: Pulse effects on the primary color to indicate "Live" status.
- **Transitions**: Smooth crossfades or cian wipes between scenes.

## What NOT to Do
- No generic blues or purples.
- No slow, linear animations.
- No cluttered layouts; keep it focused.
